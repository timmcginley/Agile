## A2 - Building System Needs

|          |  Building Systems Needs   |
|----      |-----|
|  Aim     |   Define the features of a building system (agile prototype)  |
| Tools    | Pen and paper / computational design |
| Case     | Proposed Building & Building System (for focus area) |
| Groups   | Same group of 4  |
| Practice | In class activity in week 4, 5 and 6 |
| Output   | Drawings / digital prototypes (i.e. grasshopper) |
| Submit   | Markdown file + final drawing demonstrating completion of AP1 (including 'line', 'segments' and 'features') |



## Part 1 

* Analyse proposed building of 308
* Map previous building design approaches
* Identify issues and gaps with current proposed approaches

## Part 2 

What stages could you imagine your system going through to satisfy the needs you identified in the previous assignment? 

Sequencing activity
* Determine the order in which systems grow within your building, what comes first and what follows?
* Identify and justify your sequence mapping at each step (week 5)
* Document the stages of system development with clear explanations for each transition (week 4)
* Create a stage tree diagram that illustrates the building growth stages and the role of your system within them (week 5)
* Develop a preliminary design of your system that responds to the identified needs from A1, showing how it evolves through the stages (week 6)

Submitted on DTU Learn by Sunday October 11,2026.
