# Learning Objectives

## [A1] 
* 1.1 Identify the ‘[phenotypic]’ features of a [building system] or product.
* 1.2 [Reverse engineer] the pseudo biological [developmental stages] of the [system] based on the identified [features] to transform the building system or product into an ‘[Agile Prototype]' (AP).

## [A2] 
* 2.1 Model and traverse a [phylogenetic tree] of the features of AP to identify new solutions.
* 2.2 Identify a [building system] or product that has or could address a current or emerging disciplinary problem in the architecture, engineering and construction industry.

## [A3] 
* 3.1 Extrapolate the future [features] of the AP based on its [evolution].
* 3.2 Reflect on the APs genetic future trajectory.
* 3.3 Define the three horizons of a future scenario and explore the future of the AP.
* 3.4 Adapt the AP to the three horizons of the future scenario.

## [A4] 
* 4.1 Apply [computational modelling] techniques to model the evolution of the AP.
* 4.2 Define an agile roadmap for the AP to solve the identified problem.
* 4.3 Evaluate their proposed AP.
* 4.4 Reflect on the process of developing the AP and communicate this for current and future students.
